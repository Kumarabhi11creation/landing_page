    Frequently Asked Questions

    Addressing technical feasibility, rollout realism, and campus edge cases.

    How does this operate in thick-walled hostels with patchy 2G networks?

    SAHAJ is engineered as an offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA) under a strict 85KB network budget. When connectivity drops, requests are cached in the browser's local storage and sync in the background the moment a connection is re-established.

    What is the fallback for students who genuinely don't own a smartphone?

    Students can trigger gate passes or emergency facility tickets via a two-way automated SMS shortcode service (e.g., texting 'PASS OUT' to 56070) or self-service touchscreen kiosks installed in academic foyers.

    Does our college need to abandon our existing legacy ERP or SQL database?

    No. SAHAJ contains an ingestion and sync adapter that imports student rosters, fee statuses, and course structures from existing Excel/CSV exports or SQL databases without taking systems offline.

    How does the admin command deck prevent complaint backlogs?

    Unaddressed tickets automatically escalate after a configurable duration (e.g., 24 or 48 hours). The dashboard flags recurring equipment failure hotspots by hostel wing or floor so preventive maintenance can happen before issues multiply.
